Privacy Policy — StitchTally

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how StitchTally ("the app", published as com.jamilxt.stitchtally) handles information. You are the data controller for your app's data as described below.

Summary

StitchTally works entirely on your device. Your projects, row counts, and settings are stored locally and never transmitted to us. The app has no accounts, no analytics, and no servers.

Information the app processes (on your device only)

  • Projects (name, row count, target, notes) — stored only in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting a project, using "Delete all projects", or uninstalling the app removes them permanently.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) — serves banner and interstitial advertisements. Google may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve and measure ads, including for users in the EEA with consent managed through Google's Consent Mode / user messaging platform. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and AdMob help: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase. Payment data is handled by Google Play; the app only stores a local flag of your entitlement. See https://policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions

StitchTally requires no permissions at all — no camera, location, contacts, storage, or notifications.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. If ads are configured for a release, Google Play's Families policy and AdMob's child-directed settings are complied with as applicable.

Your choices

  • Remove all ads: purchase "Remove ads" in the app (one-time).
  • Delete data: Settings → Delete all projects, or delete individual projects.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and update the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: jamilxt@gmail.com
